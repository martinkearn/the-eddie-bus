## Edwin Mowray 1942–2022
It is with sadness that we have to inform you that Eddie Mowbray — a kingpin of the Bromsgrove Minibus Appeal — died in the early hours of Tuesday 25th May 2022.

Eddie had been a driver for many years and latterly had taken over responsibility for arranging the bookings and allocating drivers. He will be very much missed. A true gentleman and friend.

## About Us
The Bromsgrove Minibus Appeal has been a charity, registered charity number 516666, since 1985.
It was established to provide transport for the residents of care homes and specialist groups in the Bromsgrove Area.

It has been run by a team of volunteers since its inception, with volunteer drivers providing the service.
The new minibus, acquired in 2020, is a Peugeot Boxer which was purchased after extensive fundraising during 2019.

We are grateful to those trusts and individuals who helped us to raise the funds.

## The present minibus
This is our present minibus which goes under the name of The EDDIE Bus, in recognition of the work done by Eddie Mowbray as a driver and organiser for many years.

## What we do
We provide transport services for care homes and specialist groups in the Bromsgrove Area, taking residents to venues such as ten pin bowling, lunches or local garden centres.

The vehicle is fitted with a lift for easy access for those with walking difficulties.
