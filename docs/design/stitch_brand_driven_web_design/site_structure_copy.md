------

## **Booking process**

1. Contact us by email or phone with your preferred date, destination and approximate passenger numbers.
1. Let us know about any accessibility requirements if you already know them, such as wheelchair users, powered wheelchairs, walking aids or passengers who may need extra support.
1. We will check whether the bus and a volunteer driver are available.
1. Once confirmed, we will agree the journey details, collection point and timings.
1. After the trip, mileage will be confirmed and payment details will be provided.

Accessibility details do not need to be finalised at the point of booking, but it is helpful to share as much information as possible so we can plan safely.

## **Hire charge**

The hire charge for 2026 is £1.25 per mile.

Mileage is calculated from the bus storage area at Upton Warren (B61 7EZ), to your collection point(s), then on to your destination,, back to the drop off point(s) and finally back to the storage area after the journey.

There is a minimum charge of £25.

The bus has a Disability Blue Badge.

------

## **Payment**

Payment is by bank transfer only.

Payment is made after the trip, once the final mileage has been confirmed. We will send an invoice.

Payment is expected within one month of the journey.

------

## **Cancellations**

Bookings can be cancelled without charge if at least 24 hours’ notice is given.

If a booking is cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice, a nominal charge of £25 will be due.

There is no charge if the trip is cancelled by the EDDIE Bus, for example because of vehicle problems or driver availability.

Cancellations due to bad weather are generally accepted without charge.

------

## **Where the bus can travel**

The EDDIE Bus is intended for day trips and group outings.

As a general guideline, trips should usually be no more than 100 miles each way. Longer journeys may be considered, so please contact us to discuss your plans.

Trips should normally take place during the daytime. Evening trips are not generally offered.

All trips must be completed within a single day. Overnight trips are not available.

Motorway journeys are allowed.

------

## **When the bus is available**

The bus is available every day except for the period from Christmas Eve up to New Year's Day.

All bookings depend on the availability of the bus and a volunteer driver.

Please contact us to discuss your preferred date and requirements.

# **About Us**

## **Heading**

About The Bromsgrove Minibus Appeal Fund

## **Intro copy**

The Bromsgrove Minibus Appeal Fund is a local registered charity that provides accessible minibus transport for elderly and disabled residents of Bromsgrove, nearby villages and surrounding areas.

The charity is run by volunteer trustees and volunteer drivers, with the aim of helping local residents enjoy days out, social activities and community trips as part of a group.

Registered charity number: 516666

------

## **Our aim**

Our aim is to provide accessible minibus transport, supported by volunteer drivers, for elderly and disabled residents of Bromsgrove.

The service enables eligible groups to get out and about safely, whether they are visiting local venues, attending group activities or enjoying days out together.

------

## **What the service provides**

We provide transport for care homes and specialist groups in the Bromsgrove area. Typical outings include:

- Lunches
- Garden centre visits
- Ten pin bowling
- Social activities
- Local community venues
- Group days out

The vehicle is fitted with a passenger lift to support people with walking difficulties and wheelchair users.

------

## **Our history**

The charity was established in 1985 after local people recognised that elderly residents in Bromsgrove would benefit from dedicated transport to help them get out and about.

Funds were raised to purchase and operate a minibus, and a charity was set up to manage the service. Originally known as Bromsgrove Minibus for the Elderly, the service has continued for decades, supported by volunteer drivers and trustees.

In 2019, the existing minibus needed to be replaced. Thanks to grants from the National Lottery, charitable groups, council grants, local businesses, service users and the charity’s own reserves, a brand-new minibus was purchased.

The new minibus was officially opened in January 2020.

------

## **The EDDIE Bus**

The current vehicle is a Peugeot Boxer 13-seat minibus, specially adapted with an electric side step and a tail lift capable of loading wheelchairs.

Some rear seats can be removed to accommodate up to three static wheelchairs. There is also storage at the back of the bus for collapsible wheelchairs and walking aids.

The name EDDIE stands for:

Everyday Drives for the Disabled, Independent and Elderly

The name also recognises the many years of service given to the charity by Eddie Mowbray, one of its early drivers.

------

## **Remembering Eddie Mowbray**

Eddie Mowbray was a central figure in the Bromsgrove Minibus Appeal and gave many years of service to the charity.

He served as a driver and later took responsibility for arranging bookings and allocating drivers. Eddie passed away in May 2022 and is remembered with gratitude and affection by the charity.

------

## **Insurance and operating standards**

The EDDIE Bus has public liability insurance and vehicle insurance in place.

The service operates in compliance with Section 19 permit requirements, which apply to community transport services of this kind.

# **Passenger & Accessibility Policy**

## **Heading**

Passenger and accessibility policy

## **Intro copy**

The EDDIE Bus is an accessible minibus that can carry able-bodied passengers, passengers who transfer from wheelchairs, static wheelchair users and, in some circumstances, powered wheelchair users.

The final decision about safe passenger numbers, wheelchair storage and seating arrangements is made by the driver.

------

## **Standard seating**

### **Maximum capacity**

13 passengers

The bus has 11 passenger seats in the main body of the vehicle, plus 2 seats at the front with the driver.

------

## **Wheelchair transfers**

### **Maximum capacity**

Up to 6 passengers who transfer from wheelchairs or walking aids, plus up to 7 able-bodied passengers.

Passengers who can transfer from a wheelchair or walking aid may be seated in the bus. Their wheelchairs are then folded and stored at the rear of the vehicle.

Passengers using walking supports are counted within the transfer passenger total.

------

## **Static wheelchairs**

### **Maximum capacity**

Up to 3 static wheelchair users, plus additional passengers depending on the final seating arrangement.

A static wheelchair is one that remains occupied while the passenger is on the minibus.

Static wheelchairs must be secured to the floor of the bus, with appropriate wheelchair restraints and seat belts fitted by the driver. At least one seat needs to be removed for each static wheelchair and stored at the client’s pickup point.

In addition to static wheelchair users, up to 3 transfer passengers may also be accommodated, allowing for up to 7 additional able-bodied passengers.

Some passengers may be seated next to the window in a more restricted position. This may not be suitable for passengers who need more active support.

------

## **Powered wheelchairs**

### **Maximum capacity**

Usually 1 powered wheelchair user, plus additional passengers depending on the final seating arrangement.

In general, only one powered wheelchair can be accommodated because of loading and space constraints.

The powered wheelchair must be secured at the front and rear to the floor of the bus, with a seat belt fitted. Two seats need to be removed and stored at the pickup point.

In addition to the powered wheelchair user, up to 3 transfer passengers may also be accommodated, allowing for up to 7 additional able-bodied passengers.

If space permits, one static wheelchair may also be accommodated in addition to the powered wheelchair, but this is at the driver’s discretion.

------

## **Important safety note**

The passenger numbers shown above are maximum figures. Actual capacity may be lower depending on the size and type of wheelchairs, walking aids and other equipment.

Large wheelchairs may significantly reduce the storage space available at the rear of the bus.

The driver has final responsibility for deciding what can be safely carried and stored.

------

## **Driver responsibilities**

The driver is responsible for:

- Driving the vehicle safely
- Meeting legal vehicle requirements
- Operating the tail lift safely
- Securing wheelchairs into the vehicle
- Deciding what can be safely carried and stored

The driver is not responsible for transferring passengers to or from the lift, transferring passengers into or out of seats, providing personal care, or providing additional passenger support.

------

## **Carer responsibilities**

Carers are responsible for:

- The general safety and behaviour of passengers
- Supporting passengers to and from the vehicle
- Transferring passengers into and out of seats
- Ensuring all seated passengers wear the seat belts fitted to their seats
- Accompanying passengers who use the tail lift
- Ensuring wheelchair brakes are applied before the lift is operated
- Providing the appropriate carer-to-passenger ratio
- Providing any extra support required by passengers

There should always be at least one carer with each group. Two carers are preferred where possible, especially where passengers need additional support.

All carers and support must be provided by the booking organisation.
