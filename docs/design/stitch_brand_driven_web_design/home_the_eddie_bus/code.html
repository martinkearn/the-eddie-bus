The Bromsgrove Minibus Appeal Fund (The EDDIE Bus) has been providing vital community transport for 40 years, ensuring mobility and social connection for our local residents.

Helping the Bromsgrove community daily

Dedicated to Accessibility

We provide reliable transport solutions specifically designed for those with mobility challenges. Our service is more than just a bus; it's a lifeline to social interaction.

accessible

Full Passenger Lift

Every trip is facilitated by our integrated rear passenger lift, allowing wheelchair users and those with limited mobility to board with ease and dignity.

groups

Group Travel

The EDDIE Bus is specifically arranged for group outings, making it perfect for care home excursions or local club visits.

"The EDDIE Bus has changed how our residents interact with the community. The accessibility is unmatched."
