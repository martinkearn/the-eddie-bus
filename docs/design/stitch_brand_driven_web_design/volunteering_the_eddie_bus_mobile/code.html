Volunteer with the EDDIE Bus

The EDDIE Bus depends on volunteers. Our drivers and helpers make it possible for elderly and disabled residents of Bromsgrove to enjoy safe, comfortable group outings.

Become a volunteer driver

We need volunteer drivers to help meet local demand. You may be suitable if you have:

check_circle A valid driving licence
check_circle 1-2 days per week available
check_circle A friendly and patient approach

Important Requirements

  • Drivers aged over 70 require D1 entitlement and a medical declaration.
  • Trial drives and inductions are provided.
  • Insurance is provided by The EDDIE Bus.
