About The Bromsgrove Minibus Appeal Fund

The Bromsgrove Minibus Appeal Fund is a local registered charity that provides accessible minibus transport for elderly and disabled residents.

Celebrating 40 Years

Established in 1985, our charity has been a cornerstone of the Bromsgrove community for decades, helping residents stay connected and active.

Originally known as Bromsgrove Minibus for the Elderly, the service was born from local recognition that dedicated transport would fundamentally change lives. In 2020, we celebrated a new chapter with the arrival of our current modern vehicle.
