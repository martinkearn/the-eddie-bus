Book the EDDIE Bus for your group outing

The EDDIE Bus is available for eligible groups of Bromsgrove residents who need accessible transport for days out, social activities and community visits.

The 5-Step Process

1
contact_page

Initial Contact

Contact us by email or phone with your preferred date, destination and approximate passenger numbers.

2
accessible

Access Requirements

Let us know about any accessibility requirements such as wheelchair users, walking aids or passengers needing extra support.

3
event_available

Availability Check

We will check whether the bus and a volunteer driver are available for your requested date and time.

4
route

Journey Details

Once confirmed, we will agree the specific journey details, collection points and pickup timings.

5
payments

Payment & Mileage

After the trip, mileage will be confirmed and an invoice sent for payment via bank transfer.

Hire Charges (2026)

£1.25 per mile
£25 minimum

Mileage is calculated from the bus storage area at Upton Warren (B61 7EZ), to your collection point, destination, and back to storage.
