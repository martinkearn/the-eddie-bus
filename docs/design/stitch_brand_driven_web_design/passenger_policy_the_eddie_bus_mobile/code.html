Passenger & Accessibility Policy

Ensuring safe, inclusive, and reliable community transport for everyone in Bromsgrove.

airline_seat_recline_extra

Standard Seating

The EDDIE Bus provides 16 high-quality, comfortable seats with safety belts. All seats are accessible via low-profile steps with sturdy handrails for those with limited mobility.

High Visibility Safety Belts
transfer_within_a_station

Wheelchair Transfers

Passengers who prefer to transfer to a standard seat can do so. Our lift provides easy boarding, and the wheelchair will be safely stowed in the designated luggage area during transit.

accessible

Static Wheelchairs

We can accommodate up to 3 static wheelchairs simultaneously. These are secured using industry-standard four-point tie-down systems and lap/diagonal belts for the passenger.

Up to 3 Slots 4-Point Secure
electric_rickshaw

Powered Wheelchairs

Due to weight and space constraints, we can usually accommodate one large powered wheelchair or scooter per trip. Please specify the model and dimensions when booking to ensure a perfect fit on our tail-lift.

warning

Important Safety Note

All wheelchairs must be in a safe, roadworthy condition. Our drivers reserve the right to refuse transport if a mobility aid cannot be safely secured using our standard equipment.

person Driver Responsibilities

  • • Operating the hydraulic tail-lift safely.
  • • Securing wheelchairs and passengers.
  • • Assisting with entry and exit from the vehicle.

groups Carer Responsibilities

  • • Assisting with personal care during the journey.
  • • Helping passengers to and from the bus door.
  • • Managing any specialized medical equipment.
