Accessible group transport for Bromsgrove care homes and community groups

The EDDIE Bus provides friendly, accessible minibus transport for residential groups, retired activity groups and specialist groups across Bromsgrove and surrounding areas.

What we do

Our volunteer-run minibus helps passengers get out and about, whether they are visiting a garden centre, going for lunch, or attending a social activity. The vehicle is fitted with a passenger lift, supporting those with walking difficulties and wheelchair users.

The EDDIE Bus serves Bromsgrove and its surrounding villages. We help local residents stay connected to their community.
