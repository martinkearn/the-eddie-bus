About The Bromsgrove Minibus Appeal Fund

Providing accessible, affordable transport to the Bromsgrove community for over four decades. We are a registered charity dedicated to keeping local people mobile and connected.

history

Our History

Established in 1985, The Bromsgrove Minibus Appeal Fund was born from a simple yet vital need: to provide reliable transportation for elderly and disabled members of our community who found traditional public transport inaccessible.

What started as a single vehicle has grown into a cornerstone of local life. Through dedicated fundraising and community support, we have successfully operated several generations of vehicles, culminating in our latest state-of-the-art bus commissioned in 2020.

40 Years of Service

Celebrating four decades of unwavering commitment to Bromsgrove (1985 - 2025).

The EDDIE Bus

Our beloved vehicle carries a name with deep local roots. The "EDDIE Bus" was named in affectionate memory of Eddie Mowbray, a founding member and tireless advocate for the Appeal Fund. Eddie's vision for a connected Bromsgrove remains at the heart of everything we do today.

Insurance & Standards

Your safety is our absolute priority. We operate strictly under Section 19 Permits (Transport Act 1985), ensuring we meet all legal requirements for non-profit transport.

  • check_circle Comprehensive Public Liability Insurance
  • check_circle Fully trained and vetted volunteer drivers
  • check_circle Regular professional vehicle safety inspections

Donations & Support

As a registered charity, we rely on the generosity of our community to keep the wheels turning. All donations go directly towards vehicle maintenance, fuel, and ensuring we can keep our booking fees affordable for everyone.
