Our service is built on the kindness of local people. Join our team and help provide vital transport for the Bromsgrove community.

Become a Volunteer Driver

Our drivers are the heartbeat of our service. You don't just drive a bus; you provide a lifeline to those who might otherwise be isolated.

Requirements

  • check_circle Valid UK driving licence (Category B or D1 depending on vehicle).
  • check_circle A friendly, patient, and approachable manner with passengers.
  • check_circle Confidence driving a large vehicle (training is provided).
  • check_circle Reliability and a commitment to helping the Bromsgrove community.
Join our team of dedicated drivers

Driver Checks & Training

We ensure all our volunteers are well-prepared and comfortable. Our current policy does not require a formal DBS check, but we maintain high standards of safety.

What we provide:

"Full inductions are provided for every new driver, covering vehicle operation, safety procedures, and passenger care."

Why Volunteer?

Volunteering with the EDDIE Bus isn't just about transport; it's about community connection. For many of our passengers, a trip on the bus is their only social outing of the week.
