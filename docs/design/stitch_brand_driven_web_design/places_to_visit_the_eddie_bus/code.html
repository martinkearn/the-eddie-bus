Places to visit with the EDDIE Bus

Our accessible community minibus is available for group hire to explore the best attractions Bromsgrove and the surrounding counties have to offer. From tranquil garden centres to historic stately homes, the EDDIE Bus is your ticket to local adventure.

Accessible for Everyone

Our bus is designed with your comfort and safety in mind. It features a rear passenger lift, flexible seating for wheelchair users, and dementia-friendly signage.
