Book the EDDIE Bus for your group outing

Providing accessible and affordable group travel for the Bromsgrove community since 1985.

event_available

Simple 5-Step Booking

1

Check Availability

Contact our booking coordinator to see if your desired date is free. Remember, we rely on our wonderful volunteer drivers!

2

Details Submission

Provide the destination, pickup time, and expected duration of your outing. Let us know if you need any special accessibility accommodations.

3

Driver Assignment

Our team will reach out to our pool of volunteers. Once a driver is confirmed, we'll send you a formal booking confirmation.

4

The Trip

Your driver will arrive at the scheduled pickup point. Please ensure your group is ready to depart on time to maximize your day.

5

Final Payment

After the trip, we calculate the mileage and issue an invoice for payment via bank transfer.

Eligibility & Carers

Who can book?

The EDDIE Bus is available for community groups, charities, and elderly resident associations within the Bromsgrove district.

Carer Responsibilities

Group organisers must provide sufficient carers for passengers requiring assistance. Drivers are responsible for the vehicle only.

Hire Charges

Rate per Mile £1.25
Minimum Charge £25.00
account_balance Payment Details

Payment is preferred via Bank Transfer. Bank details will be provided on your final invoice.

notification_important

Cancellations

We understand plans change. However, please provide at least 24 hours notice for any cancellations.

Late cancellations may be subject to a nominal administrative fee to cover our coordination costs.

Note on Availability

As a community charity, The EDDIE Bus is entirely operated by dedicated volunteers. All bookings are subject to driver availability. We recommend booking as far in advance as possible to ensure your outing can go ahead.
