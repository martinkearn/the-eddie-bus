Edwin Mowray 1942-2022

 

It is with sadness that we have to inform that Eddie Mowbray - a kingpin of the Bromsgrove Mini bus Appeal - died in the early hours of Tuesday 25th May 2022. Eddie had been a driver for many years and latterly had taken over responsibility for arranging the bookings and allocating drivers. He will be very much missed. A true gentle man and friend.
